# HiPEAC 2027

[HiPEAC 2027](https://www.hipeac.net/2027/glasgow/#/) will feature the [*ODE4EC: Open Design Environment for European Chips*](https://www.hipeac.net/2027/glasgow/#/program/8328/) workshop.

This is the second edition of the ODE4EC HiPEAC workshop, and it will provide the first public opportunity to discuss the technical directions, priorities, and early activities of the ODE4EC projects.

The event will bring together researchers, tool developers, industrial stakeholders, startups, SMEs, and representatives of the broader open-source hardware community to discuss progress, identify remaining barriers, and foster collaboration across the European semiconductor ecosystem. Beyond presenting ongoing work, the workshop will serve as a forum for gathering feedback from potential users and contributors, ensuring that future developments remain aligned with both industrial requirements and research needs.
