# Privacy

This website sets no cookies and does not track you across visits or across other websites. This page explains what data is still processed when you visit it.

## Who is responsible

The website is run by the [FOSSi Foundation](https://www.fossi-foundation.org) on behalf of the ODE4EC projects. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to [stefan@fossi-foundation.org](mailto:stefan@fossi-foundation.org).

## Hosting

The website is served by Cloudflare, Inc. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the details of each request (the page, time, browser and the page you came from) in order to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Visitor statistics

To know how many people read the website and which pages, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. When a page loads, a small script sends Cloudflare the address of the page, the page you came from, your browser and operating system type, your country and how fast the page loaded. It uses no cookies or other storage on your device and does not identify you or follow you across visits.

We only keep and publish monthly totals of page views and visits per section of the website, on the [statistics page](/stats/); these contain no personal data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used, which we report to the projects' funders (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time, for example by blocking `static.cloudflareinsights.com` in your browser.

## Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare processes data for us under a data processing agreement. Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States and is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, so transfers to it are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

## Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and keep them no longer than needed for that.

## Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live.
