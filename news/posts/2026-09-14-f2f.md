# First joint face-to-face meeting for ODE4EC

On 14-15 September 2026, the ODE4EC-AMS, ODE4EC-DIG and ODE4EC-PIV consortia gathered in Ghent, Belgium, for their first joint face-to-face meeting.

The two days focused on sharing knowledge, aligning activities and discussing the next steps for each project. Meeting together also gave partners the opportunity to explore collaboration across their complementary areas:

🔹 ODE4EC-AMS – analogue and mixed-signal design

🔹 ODE4EC-DIG – digital system-on-chip design

🔹 ODE4EC-PIV – productivity, interoperability and verification

Alongside the technical discussions, the meeting gave colleagues time to get to know one another, exchange perspectives and build the relationships that will support our work across the three projects.

Thank you to all partners for their contributions and constructive discussions, and to [FOSSi Foundation](https://fossi-foundation.org/) for hosting us. We look forward to turning these exchanges into continued collaboration.
