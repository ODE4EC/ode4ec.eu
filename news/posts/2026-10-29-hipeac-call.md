# Call for lightning talks: ODE4EC Workshop at HiPEAC 2027

**Tuesday, 19 January 2027 · 10:00–17:30 (local time, GMT) · Glasgow, UK**
**Submission deadline: 15 November 2026**

What are you building for open chip design? We want to hear about it.

<img src="/img/2026-10-29-hipeac-call-flyer.png" alt="Call for lightning talks: ODE4EC Workshop at HiPEAC 2027, 19 January 2027, Glasgow, UK. Submission deadline 15 November 2026." style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 440px; margin: 1.5rem auto;" />

The workshop *ODE4EC: Open Design Environment for European Chips* at HiPEAC 2027 brings together the three ODE4EC projects and the wider open-source chip design community. We invite lightning talks from everyone who builds, uses or teaches open-source EDA tools and open PDKs.

## Topics

- Open-source EDA tools and design flows
- Design productivity, interoperability, verification and AI-assisted hardware design
- Open PDKs, MPW runs and tape-out experiences
- Education and training with open tools
- Industrial adoption and collaborative projects
- Early-stage ideas and work in progress

## Who can submit

We welcome contributions from academia, industry, start-ups and open-source communities. PhD students and early-career researchers are especially encouraged to submit. You do not need finished results: early ideas and work in progress are welcome.

## Format

- Lightning talks are given in person in Glasgow.
- The length of each talk will be set based on the number of submissions.

## Important dates

- Submission deadline: **15 November 2026**
- Notification of acceptance: **30 November 2026**
- Workshop: **19 January 2027**

## How to submit

Send us a 100-word abstract of your talk through the submission form:

[Submit your proposal](https://forms.gle/23Majnfn5sg5X9xq8)

## The ODE4EC projects

- [ODE4EC-AMS](/projects/ams/) – analogue and mixed-signal design
- [ODE4EC-DIG](/projects/dig/) – digital system-on-chip design
- [ODE4EC-PIV](/projects/piv/) – productivity, interoperability and verification
